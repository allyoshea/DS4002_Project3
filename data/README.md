# Metadata 
## Data Summary 

Our dataset contains images of 12 marine organism categories, including marine mammals, fish, invertebrates, a sea turtle, and kelp. We use these images to train and evaluate a MobileNetV2 classifier, with a target test accuracy of at least 90%. Images are the model inputs, and category folder names provide the target labels. Taxon IDs, locations, and contributor information are metadata, not predictors.

## Provenance
Ally O’Shea, Carter Grohe, and Coco Zhao collected the dataset from November 4 to November 13, 2026, as part of their DS 4002 project at the University of Virginia. Python was used to download observation records and photos from the iNaturalist API.

## Ethical Statements
This project uses publicly available observations for educational purposes. The metadata may still include wildlife locations but we do not use this information as model inputs.
