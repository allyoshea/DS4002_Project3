# Metadata 
## Data Summary 

Our dataset contains iNaturalist images of 12 marine organism categories, including marine mammals, fish, invertebrates, a sea turtle, and kelp. We use these images to train and evaluate a MobileNetV2 classifier, with a target test accuracy of at least 90%. Images are the model inputs, and category folder names provide the target labels. Taxon IDs, locations, and contributor information are metadata, not predictors.
